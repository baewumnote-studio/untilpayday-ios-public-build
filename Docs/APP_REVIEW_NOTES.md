# App Review Notes — Payday Left

Payday Left is an offline paycheck-based budgeting utility.

No account or login is required. The app does not connect to a bank or financial institution and does not move, hold or manage money.

## Review path

1. Launch and complete onboarding.
2. Choose any pay schedule and enter a sample amount and future payday.
3. Add an upcoming bill or protected savings amount.
4. Today shows Left Until Payday and Safe Today.
5. Add an expense to see Safe Today update.
6. Plan shows reserved bills and recurring templates.
7. History shows completed pay cycles after a cycle is closed.
8. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets use local App Group data.

Budget records are stored locally on the device.

Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform are included for advertising and applicable privacy choices. Advertising is not required for budgeting functionality.

The app intentionally does not present an ATT prompt in this version and does not directly request IDFA access.

There is no subscription or in-app purchase.

If advertising cannot load, all budgeting functionality remains available.
